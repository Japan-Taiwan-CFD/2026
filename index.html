About the Workshop

The Japan–Taiwan CFD Workshop brings together researchers from Japan and Taiwan to share recent advances in computational fluid dynamics (CFD), high-performance computing (HPC), AI, and related numerical simulations. The workshop includes technical presentations on December 21 and CFD software demonstrations and tutorials on December 22.
